# 📸 Photos (optional)

Put your photos here. They are found automatically.

- **`cover.jpg`**: the main photo at the top of the invitation (portrait photos look best)
- **`gallery/1.jpg`, `gallery/2.jpg`, `gallery/3.jpg` …**: the photo gallery. Number them with no gaps.

`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png` and `.webp` all work. Keep each photo under ~500 KB (shrink them free at https://squoosh.app).

No photos? That's fine. The invitation shows a decorated design instead.
